## [React/TypeScript/Python/Django] Texas Seguros - informativoir.com.br

### Project Overview:
Designed a CRM with Django, DRF (Django REST Framework), React, and PostgreSQL, saving the company $5 million annually. Resolved a complex historical data issue for a contract with SulAmerica, the 2nd largest Brazilian insurance company with more than 7+ million users. Engineered system modules including users, plans, taxes, and dental, ensuring data integrity and reducing processing time by 40%. Optimized database queries to reduce time of requests by 50%, and developed algorithms to calculate income tax statements.

### Achievements:

- **Designed a CRM solution that saved the company $5 million annually**
- **Resolved complex historical data issues for a contract with SulAmerica (7+ million users)**
- **Engineered system modules ensuring data integrity and reducing processing time by 40%**
- **Optimized database queries to reduce request time by 50%**

### Technologies/Tech Stack:
**React, TypeScript, Python, Django, Django REST Framework, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, SQL, Object-Oriented Programming**
